# Lab 2: Analog Input

## Potentiometer

This one stumped me for a little bit.

TL;DR - I initially wired up a *rotary encoder* instead of a *potentiometer* which caused a bug where the Arduino would intermittently disconnect as I turned the knob. 

After googling a bit, I found some forum posts ([1](https://forum.arduino.cc/t/potentiometer-turning-off-arduino/550263/9)) ([2](https://forum.arduino.cc/t/problem-with-potentiometer/539326)) of people with the same issue, and swapped out the rotary encoder for a potentiometer.
