## Selected Projects

**SoundMesh** (Master's Thesis) — *2025–2026, ongoing*

Wireless mesh-networked audio system for multi-point sound in informal and pop-up community spaces. Built on dual-core ESP32 microcontrollers streaming 48kHz/24-bit audio across a self-healing network. Selected for the NYU Startup Accelerator Program (Summer 2026).

**in the palm of your hands** — *2025*

Two-handed MIDI instrument combining tactile arcade buttons (scale degrees) and spatial time-of-flight gestures (timbre, modifier chords). Built on the Electrosmith Daisy platform with RGB and haptic feedback. NIME class project. [Portfolio link](https://justintime.art/in-the-palm-of-your-hands)

**The Sinetactophone** — *2024–2025*

Two-player collaborative instrument exploring expressive gesture. Mixes theremin-style motion control (Arduino-driven time-of-flight sensors → Ableton) with the discrete feel of physical buttons. Audio-reactive LEDs. Exhibited at IMA Shows 2024 and 2025. [Portfolio link](https://justintime.art/the-sinetactophone)

**Tangible Music Looper (DJ Remix Box)** — *2024*

Standalone physical music sequencer. Hall-effect sensors beneath a board; magnet-embedded 3D-printed blocks trigger looping audio stems via Raspberry Pi multi-threaded playback. NeoPixel LED feedback, internal amp and speakers — no laptop required. Built with Michelle Xu. [Portfolio link](https://justintime.art/tangible-music-looper)

**The Tempest arcade controller** — *2025*

Hand-made cherry-and-poplar arcade cabinet built for a custom Unity clone of the vector-graphics classic *Tempest*. Teensy 4.0, arcade buttons, rotary encoder. Part of an ongoing "symmetry is overrated" fabrication series. [Portfolio link](https://justintime.art/the-tempest-arcade-controller)
